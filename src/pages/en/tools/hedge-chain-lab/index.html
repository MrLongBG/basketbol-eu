How it works: The calculator works backward from the total combo return. It computes the Match 2 hedge first (future stake, placed only if your combo-leg wins Match 1), then subtracts it from the total to find the "target equity" — the value to protect through Match 1 hedges. All Match 1 hedges are then sized so every outcome delivers the same locked profit.
